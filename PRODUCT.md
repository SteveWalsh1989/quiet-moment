# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue 3 with the Composition API and Tailwind CSS, based on the user's established preferences.

## Users

A person using a Mac who wants practical help settling down during anxious moments.

## Product Purpose

Offer breathing and grounding exercises the visitor can start immediately, with settings for timing, spoken cues and gentle audio.

## Operating Context

The visitor can be anxious and may want to follow a countdown, read an example, listen with their eyes closed, or change exercises at any point.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site offers recommended timing plus custom timing, a visual countdown, optional voice and quiet background sound, and sensory grounding with concrete examples. The interface identifies the exercises as self-help and not medical care.

## Default flow

One click starts breathing and automatically opens grounding afterward. The two exercises can also be started directly.
